# Jungle. website design system

Source of truth: *Jungle. Brand Book v1.0 (October 2026)*. Tokens live in `assets/style.css`.

- **Colour:** Ink `#1A1A1A` leads (60%), Paper `#FFFFFF` gives space (30%), Jungle Green `#61FE87` is the spark (10%). Smoke `#9A9A9A` = secondary on Ink, Stone `#6B6B6B` = secondary on Paper, Mist `#ECECEC` = panels/rules on Paper. Green is never text, icons or thin lines on Paper.
- **Type:** PP Agrandir Regular for headlines (line-height 1.0-1.1), PP Agrandir Narrow for one voice line per layout, PP Agrandir Text Bold for numbers. Aileron Regular for body, labels and UI; Aileron Light for intros. No bold Aileron.
- **Graphic language:** the 10° slant (section edges, hover wipes, page transition), the full stop ending every headline (Green on Ink, Ink on Paper/Green; added automatically by `site.js`), one 10°-tilted sticker per layout.
- **Motion:** ease out, settle fast, 0.3-0.6s. The dot lands with one bounce; hero titles build word by word; panels wipe along the slant. All of it is disabled under reduced motion.
- **Logo:** supplied artwork only. Green dot on Ink (nav), all-Ink in the footer on Paper, Ink dot on the Green transition panel.
- **Voice:** clear, human, confident, work-first. Services mirror superside.com minus branding and production (see DISCIPLINES in site.js). Never offer branding.
